# Zettelkasten

A slip-box of small, linked notes. Value comes from the **links**, not the
individual notes: an idea becomes useful when it is connected to others.

- Write [[Atomic Notes]]: one idea per note.
- Prefer [[Evergreen Notes]] that are rewritten and improved over time.
- Origin and practice: [[How to Take Smart Notes]].
