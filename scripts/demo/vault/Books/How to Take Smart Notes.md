# How to Take Smart Notes

A practical introduction to the [[Zettelkasten]] method: write permanent notes
in your own words, link them, and let ideas grow bottom-up.

## Key ideas

- Writing is not the outcome of thinking; it is the medium in which thinking happens.
- Separate fleeting, literature, and permanent notes.
- Each note should be [[Atomic Notes|atomic]] and link to at least one other note.
- Elaboration beats re-reading; see [[Spaced Repetition]] for the memory side.

Related: [[Evergreen Notes]], [[Deep Work]].
