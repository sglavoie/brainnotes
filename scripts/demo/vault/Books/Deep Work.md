# Deep Work

Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration
push cognitive capabilities to their limit. These efforts create new value,
improve skill, and are hard to replicate.

## Key ideas

- Depth is becoming rare at exactly the moment it is becoming valuable.
- Schedule every minute of the day; protect long blocks for [[Deliberate Practice]].
- Embrace boredom: the ability to concentrate is a skill that must be trained.
- Drain the shallows: batch email and meetings into fixed windows.
