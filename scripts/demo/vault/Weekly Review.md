# Weekly Review

Every Sunday:

1. Empty the [[Inbox]] into proper notes.
2. Check active projects: [[Learn Rust]], [[Garden Planner]].
3. Block time for [[Deep Work]] next week.
4. Pick one book from the reading list: [[Atomic Habits]],
   [[Thinking, Fast and Slow]] or [[The Art of Learning]].
5. Keep reading [[The Pragmatic Programmer]].
